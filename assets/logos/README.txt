Yahan apna logo daalo (PNG format), file ka naam bilkul aisa hi rakho:

  logo.png     -> Frozetto

Tips:
- Kam se kam 300px chaudai. Transparent background theek hai.
- Logo PDF bill ke top-right corner mein aur app ke header/login mein dikhega.
- Logo na ho to bill bina logo ke banega - kuch tootega nahi.

Signature (optional):
  signature.png  (ya signature.jpg) -> bill/PDF mein "Authorized Signatory" ke upar aayega.
  Tip: safed ya transparent background par saaf signature, chaudai ~400px.
